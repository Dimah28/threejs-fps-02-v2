Ironworks Protocol

A tactical round-based FPS. Sentinels defend the Ironworks District; Vipers plant the bomb at Site A (Furnace Hall) or Site B (Crane Yard) — or eliminate the other team. First to 8 rounds takes the match.

  • WASD Move · Shift Run · Ctrl Crouch · Space Jump
  • Mouse Look · Click Fire · RMB Scope (Longbow)
  • R Reload · G Throw grenade · E Plant / defuse (hold)
  • 1–6 Weapons · B Buy menu (buy phase)
